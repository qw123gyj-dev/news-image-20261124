SPACE — THE NEW FRONTIER

Allies warn Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite

Sputnik-S could go up as soon as December, Western intelligence says; NATO vows to defend space.
Western intelligence agencies warned that Russia has built a satellite, Sputnik-S, designed to carry a nuclear warhead and could launch it as soon as next month. The warning, shared among NATO countries in early November, was first published by German media on Monday. NATO’s chief, in Berlin, vowed the alliance would defend itself in space; the Kremlin dismissed the reports.
TECH — SILICON VALLEY

Nvidia brings Huang back as chief executive

Eleven days after he quit, the founder returns — and shares jump a third.
Nvidia’s board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, 11 days after he resigned over extramarital affairs. President Trump said he pressed the board to bring him back. Shares leapt about a third; the Nasdaq rose 6% and the S&P 500 about 4.7%.
KOREA — EAST SEA

Russian jets set off a shelter alert on Ulleung Island

Warplanes enter the air defence zone; the alarm is lifted in five minutes.
Several Russian military aircraft entered South Korea’s air defence zone over the East Sea on Tuesday morning and flew towards Ulleung Island, prompting an evacuation alert at 7:52 a.m. Fighters were scrambled and the alert was lifted about five minutes later. The military said no airspace was violated.
KOREA — PYONGYANG

Pyongyang threatens to close the Swiss office

Weeks after it opened, the mission may go.
Kim Yo-jong said on Tuesday that North Korea would consider shutting Switzerland’s diplomatic office in Pyongyang, opened only weeks ago, after Bern sanctioned the Songdowon children’s camp over alleged transfers of Ukrainian children.
AMERICA — WASHINGTON

Trump orders federal executions to be expedited

Death penalty sought for child murder; Newsom attacked.
President Trump signed a memorandum on Monday directing the attorney general to seek the death penalty for child murder and murders during rape, sex trafficking or kidnapping, and to carry out federal death sentences as fast as possible.
TECH — THE COURTS

Two states sue Musk’s SpaceX over Grok sex images

First state suits over the chatbot’s “spicy mode”.
Virginia and Oregon filed the first state lawsuits against Elon Musk’s SpaceX and related companies on Monday, saying Grok’s image tools turn ordinary photos into sexualised images, including of children. A study cited in the suits counted about 3 million such images on X.
CHINA — BEIJING

China sentences an official to death for spying

Thirty-five state secrets for more than a million yuan.
China sentenced an official identified only as Ma to death for leaking state secrets to a foreign intelligence service: more than 100 intelligence reports, 35 of them state secrets, for over 1 million yuan. The court and the agency were not named.
